Transport

How to reach AgID

🚇 METRO
🚇
Metro B
From city centre · Fastest
  • 1Take Metro B (blue line) from any B station
  • 2Direction Laurentina — exit at EUR Palasport
  • 3Exit onto Viale Beethoven, walk north ~200m
  • 4Turn right into Via Liszt — AgID n. 21 on the left after ~150m
⏱ ~20–30 min from Termini € 1.50 per trip Line B
🗺️ Walking route: EUR Palasport → AgID
Step by step: Exit EUR Palasport station → walk north on Viale Beethoven (~200m) → turn right into Via Liszt → AgID is at n. 21 on your left (~150m). Total: ~350m, about 5 min.
🚆 TRAIN
🚆
From Roma Termini
High-speed or regional trains
  • 1Arrive at Roma Termini — Italy's main rail hub
  • 2Follow signs to Metro B (blue), platforms level −1
  • 3Direction Laurentina, exit at EUR Palasport (8 stops, ~17 min)
  • 4Exit onto Viale Beethoven, walk north ~200m, turn right into Via Liszt — AgID at n. 21
⏱ ~30 min total € 1.50 metro ticket

From AgID: Exit onto Via Liszt → left on Viale Beethoven (~150m) → left to EUR Palasport Metro B (~5 min walk). Board direction Jonio / Rebibbia and ride 8 stops to Colosseo. Exit: the Colosseum is visible immediately outside the station.
🏛️
Part 2 — Walking tour: Colosseo → Altare della Patria
~2 km on foot · flat terrain · ~45–60 min at a leisurely pace
Route: 1 Start at the Colosseo (exterior view, Arco di Costantino) → 2 Walk west along Via dei Fori Imperiali (~600m, ruins of the Imperial Fora on both sides) → 3 Climb the Campidoglio (Michelangelo's piazza, panoramic view over the Roman Forum) → 4 Descend to Altare della Patria / Piazza Venezia (Vittoriano monument) → 5 Return eastward along Via dei Fori Imperiali toward Colosseo for metro home (or taxis at Piazza Venezia).

☀️ Tip: Comfortable shoes essential. Bring water — June temperatures reach 30–34 °C. Free water fountains (nasoni) along the route.

Useful info

Tips for participants

📱
Navigation apps
Google Maps and Moovit work well for Rome transit. Download offline maps for the city centre.
💳
Payments
Cards and contactless widely accepted. Metro gates support Tap & Go (bank card / phone) at €1.50/ride.
☀️
June weather
Expect 28–34 °C, mostly sunny. Sunscreen, sunglasses and a water bottle essential. Free water fountains (nasoni) throughout Rome.
🛜
Connectivity
EU roaming applies across all EU countries. SIM cards at FCO airport or Termini (TIM, Vodafone, WINDTRE, Iliad).
🏨
Where to stay
Prati, Trastevere, or near Termini for good Metro B access. EUR district hotels are a short walk to AgID.
⚡
Power plugs
Italy uses Type F/L plugs, 230V. Most EU plugs fit without adapter. UK/US participants need a Type F adapter.
🍕
Lunch near AgID
Cafés and restaurants within 5 min along Viale Europa and Viale della Civiltà del Lavoro in the EUR district.
🗣️
Language
English widely understood in EUR district and tourist areas. Workshop sessions conducted in English.
